## 2. Invariant Claim Audit

* **`INV-1` (AI Key Isolation)**: `PASS` — No AI agent holds private keys.
* **`INV-2` (Server-Controlled Recipients)**: `PASS` — Destination addresses are resolved exclusively by the authoritative service registry.
* **`INV-10` (Simulation Isolation)**: `PASS` — `is_simulation = true` transactions are strictly rejected by the execution signer.
* **`INV-46` (Inviolable HARD_DENY)**: `PASS` — Sanctioned or blocked entities cannot be overridden by human approval.
* **`INV-107` (Zero Broadcast in Simulation)**: `PASS` — Simulated missions execute zero blockchain RPC broadcasts.
